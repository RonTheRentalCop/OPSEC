# OPSEC
OPSEC (Based off the meme) is an open source tool that applies a tiered security and privacy configuration to a computer with one command. (Hopefully)


## I am looking for contributers! Please DM me on Discord at courtleeoed.
    

How the tiers work:

Hygiene: Baseline Internet Saftey and Security. Prioritizing speed and some privacy for the clear web.
- Stronger Passwords
- Ad Campaigns blocked
- Updates On
- Trackers Blocked
Private: Deeper into the hole of Privacy and Security. Still Proiritizes speed but slower. More emphisis on privacy and protection of your data.
- Hidden from ISP, networks, and data collectors
- Auto Cookie Denial
- Hightened site and device security.
Hardened: Much more focus on moving to the
